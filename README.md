# ❤️‍🔥 How Do You Love?

A 30-question quiz on how you **give** and **receive** love — and what to do about it.

**[Take the quiz →](https://sqquirrelyoshi-sys.github.io/how-do-you-love/)**

---

## Why this exists

Most love language quizzes tell you how you like to be loved and stop there. That's a fine party trick and a poor formation tool.

This one asks both directions — how you receive love *and* how you give it — because the gap between them is where most misunderstanding lives. You give what you'd want. They give what they'd want. Nobody feels loved.

Then it goes one step further: your language is paired with a **work of mercy**, a **saint who loved that way**, and a ladder of things you could actually do — from grabbing food with friends to visiting people nobody visits.

> *"Children, let us love not in word or speech but in deed and truth."* — 1 John 3:18

---

## Fair warnings

- **This is not a diagnosis.** The five love languages were popularized by Gary Chapman and are not an empirically validated personality model. Research suggests we all need all five. Treat your result as a conversation and prayer starter.
- **The works of mercy pairing is a suggestion, not Church teaching.** No magisterial document maps love languages to the corporal and spiritual works of mercy. We made the connection because it's useful for prayer — love languages describe temperament, the works of mercy describe obligation.
- **Intercession isn't on here.** If praying for others is where you come alive, that's not a love language — that's likely a charism, a grace given for others (1 Cor 12:7). Different question, different tool.
- **Nothing you enter leaves your phone.** No accounts, no tracking, no data stored or sent. All quiz items are original.

## Bring it to your household

The downloaded summary ends with four questions:

1. **Give** — Where does my top giving language already show up at home?
2. **Receive** — Do I actually receive love, or deflect it?
3. **Gifts** — How might God elevate the way I naturally love into something for others?
4. **Mercy** — Which work of mercy could we actually start this quarter?

Run it as a 30-minute discussion. Everyone takes the quiz beforehand and brings their summary.

## Fork it for your community

Single file, no build step, no dependencies.

1. Fork this repo
2. Edit `index.html` — swap the activity lists in `CARD_LADDER` and `ACTIVITIES` for what your community actually does
3. Set `QUIZ_URL` to your own Pages URL
4. **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` / root**

Ideas, corrections, and better saint matches are all welcome — open an issue.
